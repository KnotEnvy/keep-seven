## 1. Mission

You turn three JSON files into a stage: 552 m of critical path, seven title cards, 152 lines
of text, four puzzles, six encounters, seventeen checkpoints, two lift rides, one ending.
Pillar 3 (**story through place**) and pillar 4 (**puzzles that use the gun and the player's
attention**) are your pillars: the narrator speaks once, at the moment the player is looking;
every puzzle is solved by shooting or by **not** shooting; nothing can soft-lock, and every
puzzle relaxes itself (hint tier 4) so a slow player still finishes inside 25 minutes. You
are also the keeper of pillar 5's memory budget: the resident-set swaps on the peg stair and
the proving lift are yours. **You never hardcode an asset id, a position or a string**:
markers, bindings and story keys are data, and `design/layout.json` is the truth.
